# Long Covid: proposed novelty test, not a result

**Current verdict:** No new named biological discovery, validated new method, or demonstrated same-task advantage. This is an unproven research direction, not a novelty claim or finished project.

## Falsifiable candidate contribution
A compartment-robust post-infection persistence signature that distinguishes long COVID from recovered infection and ME/CFS under matched time and participant definitions.

## What would establish or refute it
Freeze a new training-only feature-selection rule and a patient-level cross-assay evaluation, with identical infected-recovered target and same held-out subjects for a published comparator. Predeclare the endpoint, exclusion rules, analysis code and comparator before examining the held-out outcome; retain null and negative results. If the effect fails, the proposed contribution fails rather than becoming a post-hoc new marker.
